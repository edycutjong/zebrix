The Problem: Blind Spots

Prediction markets on Polymarket are inefficient by design.

  • The NBA publishes referee crews 24 hours before tip-off.
  • Retail traders and standard models systematically ignore officiating biases.
  • By the time the game starts, the true probability is mismatched with the market price.
Problem: In any market, an edge exists where information is public but ignored. The NBA announces refs 24 hours in advance. But prediction market traders are focused on injuries and player matchups. They completely ignore that certain refs significantly skew the game flow. This creates a massive pricing mismatch.

The Workflow

  1. Ingest: Scrape `official.nba.com` for daily referee assignments.
  2. Enrich: Cross-reference with 6+ seasons of historical tendency data.
  3. Score: Calculate the "Referee Adjustment Factor".
  4. Signal: Compare model fair price vs Polymarket CLOB.
  5. Execute: Trigger Canon CLI (DEGA) to place limits orders on Polygon.
How it Works: It's a fully automated pipeline. We scrape the official NBA site the minute assignments drop. We cross-reference those names against a 6-year database of tendencies. We calculate a composite score, check the Polymarket Central Limit Order Book, and if the edge is there, our Canon CLI integration fires off the trades on the Polygon network.

Risk Management

Protecting the bankroll systematically.

  • Max Single Trade: 5% of Bankroll
  • Max Exposure: 20% of Bankroll
  • Edge Threshold: Minimum 6% required
  • Position Sizing: Half-Kelly fractional
  • Stop-Loss: Auto-exit at -15%
Risk: Any good trading bot lives and dies by risk management. We hardcode strict limits: 5% max on a single trade, 20% max total exposure. We use Half-Kelly fractional sizing to optimize growth without over-leveraging, and we have hard stop-losses in place.
